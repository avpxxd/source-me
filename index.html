General Fact-Checking

Essential organizations and tools dedicated to verifying claims and debunking rumors.

  • Snopes

    One of the oldest and most trusted fact-checking sites on the internet, specializing in urban legends, rumors, and viral misinformation.

  • PolitiFact

    Focuses on looking at specific statements made by politicians and rating them for accuracy using their famous "Truth-O-Meter".

  • FactCheck.org

    A nonpartisan, nonprofit project of the Annenberg Public Policy Center that aims to reduce the level of deception and confusion in U.S. politics.

  • Reuters Fact Check

    Operated by Reuters, an international media agency, providing robust fact-checking of global news and social media claims.

Media & Image Verification

Tools to check if images or videos have been manipulated, altered, or used out of context.

  • TinEye Reverse Image Search

    An essential tool for verifying images. Upload a picture or paste a URL to see where else it appears online and find its original source and date.

  • Google Fact Check Explorer

    Search a specific topic to see if it has already been debunked or verified by independent fact-checking organizations worldwide.

  • InVID Verification Plugin

    A powerful browser extension used by journalists and researchers to analyze videos and images, extract metadata, and identify deepfakes.

Education & Digital Literacy

Resources to build critical thinking skills for students, parents, and the general public.

  • MediaSmarts: Break the Fake

    Canadian educational materials, tip sheets, and videos designed to teach critical thinking and how to spot suspicious posts.

  • UNICEF: Spotting Misinformation

    A quick guide from UNICEF on how to identify fake news, understand the emotional tactics used, and how to have conversations with others about it.
